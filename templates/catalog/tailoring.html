Taking Your Own Measurements

Measurement Guide

Not sure how? Leave the notes blank on the form and our team will guide you by phone.

↔
Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under the arms.

↔
Shoulder

Measure from the edge of one shoulder to the other, across the back.

↔
Sleeve Length

From shoulder edge to wrist bone, with arm slightly bent.

↔
Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, keeping the tape snug.

↔
Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips.

↔
Inseam / Length

From crotch to ankle bone for trousers, or shoulder to hem for agbada/kaftan.
